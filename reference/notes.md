# Style reference (added 13 Nov 2026)

Seven frames supplied by the channel owner as the target look for future videos.

What they have in common:
- Anime-style illustration: clean dark outlines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone, detailed faces and hands.
- Muted, cool palette (navy, charcoal, grey) with one warm accent per frame (orange jumpsuit, desk lamp, red display).
- Mid or close framing on one to three characters; simple, slightly blurred interior backgrounds.
- Captions: two or three words at a time, bold rounded sans-serif, white with ONE word in red, centred, soft dark glow behind.

Source frames are vertical (9:16). Long videos need the same look in wide 16:9.
