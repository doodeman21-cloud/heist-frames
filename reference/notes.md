# Style reference (added 13 Nov 2026)

Seven frames supplied by the channel owner as the target look for future videos.

What they have in common:
- Anime-style illustration: clean dark outlines, flat cel shading with one shadow tone, detailed faces and hands.
- Muted, cool palette (navy, charcoal, grey) with one warm accent per frame (orange jumpsuit, desk lamp, red display).
- Mid or close framing on one to three characters; simple, slightly blurred interior backgrounds.
- Captions: two or three words at a time, bold rounded sans-serif, white with ONE word in red, centred, soft dark glow behind.

Source frames are vertical (9:16). Long videos need the same look in wide 16:9.

## What two full reference Shorts show (measured 13 Nov 2026)

Two Shorts supplied by the channel owner, 70.7 s and 69.0 s, both 1080x1920.

- Images: about 16 per Short, so a new image every 4 to 5 seconds on average (shortest about 1.5 s, longest about 9 s).
- Movement: each image is a still with a slow zoom; hard cuts between images, no fancy transitions.
- Captions: two words on screen at a time, changing roughly every half second. The word being spoken turns red, the other stays white. Bold rounded capitals, dark glow, centred just above the middle of the frame. Numbers are spelled out as words.
- Characters: the main character keeps the same face, hair and clothes from scene to scene.
- Structure: the headline claim lands in the first two seconds, the story runs in time order, and it ends on the emotional or surprising beat.

For a 3 to 4 minute video at this pace: about 45 to 50 images.
